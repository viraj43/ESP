🔍 How It Works

Lead ESP Analysis: Fetches leads from your database and categorizes them by ESP type, reply status, and provides detailed breakdowns.

Primary Replies Analysis: Analyzes primary email replies (emode_focused) within a specific date range with full ESP lookup and IST timezone conversion.

Other Replies Analysis: Analyzes other/secondary email replies (emode_others) within a date range without ESP lookup for faster processing.

Campaign Replies Analysis: Finds campaigns in date range, fetches replies for each specific campaign, and provides ESP breakdown with campaign-wise statistics.

Combined Replies Analysis: Legacy option that combines both primary and other replies analysis in one process.

Event Type Analysis: Fetches campaigns within a date range, analyzes their activity data, and creates a matrix showing event type counts per subsequence/step.

Campaign Lead Analysis: Analyzes campaigns within a date range, fetches all leads for each campaign, and tracks contact status (status_summary) with ESP breakdown for contacted leads only.

Campaign Sent Analytics: Uses Instantly API to fetch campaign analytics data within a date range, providing email sent counts per campaign with total aggregation and Excel export.

Email Touch Analysis: Searches custom tags, fetches tagged accounts, and analyzes campaign touch data to track email sent counts per account with daily limits and health scores.
